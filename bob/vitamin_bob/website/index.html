What if the clinic’s front desk lived inside a missed call?

Not an app. Not a smartphone. The call anyone can already make, answered by a small AI that listens, sorts and routes, so the scarce clinician’s time goes to the people who need it most.

1/8How Bob helps

She rings once and hangs up. It costs her nothing.

A missed call never connects, so the caller pays nothing. A laptop at the district hub sees it within seconds and queues a callback.
  • ▸Works on any phone: no app, no data plan
  • ▸The district pays for the callback

3/8How Bob helps

She describes the problem once, in her own words.

Up to 25 seconds of speech. A small model on one laptop turns it into text, then into a fixed form of symptoms and danger signs.
  • ▸Gemma 4 runs on the district laptop: no internet
  • ▸A 25-second clip is understood in under 20 seconds

Source: Measured on the demo laptop (RTX 3050 Ti, 4 GB). Gemma 4 E2B, 8-bit, via llama.cpp.

4/8How Bob helps

Every answer must be backed by her words.

The model only fills the form. If it cannot point to the words she used, the answer stays unknown, and Bob asks a simple yes-or-no question on the keypad.
  • ▸No quote, no answer: it is asked on the keypad instead
  • ▸What she already said is not asked again

5/8How Bob helps

Written rules decide. Not sure? Ask a person.

The level comes from transparent rules drafted from WHO danger signs, never from the model’s opinion. When an answer is missing, the case becomes UNCERTAIN and a clinician decides.
  • ▸Five levels of urgency, each with its reasons
  • ▸Anything that could hide an emergency goes to a clinician

6/8How Bob helps

The nearest clinic that is actually open.

Each village has its clinics in order of travel time. Bob picks the first that is open and has room, and tells her which one, by name.
  • ▸Clinics report OPEN, CLOSED or FULL by SMS or a free missed call
  • ▸Emergencies always go to 108

7/8How Bob helps

Urgent cases land on a clinician’s phone.

  • ▸No answer in 3 minutes: it moves to the next clinic, then the district
