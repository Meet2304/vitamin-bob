The solution

One free missed call.
The right care, at the right time.

For Noor

Knows what to do in one call

Call 108, go to the nearest open clinic now, or come at a booked time. Free, on any phone, in her own language.

For the clinic

Urgent cases first, story already written

Patients arrive sorted by need, with their symptoms recorded. Less paperwork for scarce staff.

For the district

One laptop, no internet

A small AI model runs on the laptop and sends patients to the nearest clinic that is open.

How it works

Five steps, about three minutes

1

Free missed call

Noor calls and hangs up. It costs her nothing.

2

Bob calls back

In Hindi or Gujarati, on any phone.

3

She describes it

Gemma 4, on the laptop, turns her words into a checklist.

4

Only what's missing

Keypad questions: 1 yes, 2 no, 3 don't know.

5

Rules decide

Call 108, go now, or a booked slot. A clinician confirms urgent cases.

The AI never speaks and never decides the urgency.
It only listens and fills in the form.
