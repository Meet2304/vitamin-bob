Receiving phone

Demonstration caller

This phone places the missed call, receives the callback and receives the patient summary.

Must not be a registered clinician or the district contact: Bob treats a clinician’s missed call as a check-in, not a patient.
3 · Clinician

Receiving alerts

This phone receives clinician summaries and alerts for the selected clinic.

Leave name and number empty to run without a clinician: the demo still works; clinician SMS is unavailable.
Restart required to apply. Active calls are never interrupted.

SMS summaries

These are SMS messages, not app or browser push notifications, which do not exist. “Configured” above means a number is set, not that delivery has been tested.

Which clinic reaches a real phone

The clinician receives alerts only for the clinic selected above. Cases routed to other clinics go to their placeholder numbers. Alerts can always be acknowledged on the operations dashboard; acknowledgement by SMS has not been demonstrated yet.
